EXCEL AND PRODUCTS

Find the country for each code.

Upload a workbook with product codes and enter the website URL. The download keeps your workbook layout and fills the country column you choose.

Search details

The active sheet of an .xlsx file is used. Maximum: 2,000 codes.

Codes may be numbers or text. Store codes as text in Excel to keep leading zeros.

Use a homepage, search page, or product URL containing {code}.

Leave blank to look for “Kodi”, “Code”, or “SKU”; otherwise column A is used.

Enter an existing column name or letter. Leave blank to create a new “Country” column.

Choose the language used for country names in the workbook.

PRODUCT DETAILS

Find ingredients by product code.

Enter product codes and a website URL. Image Find reads the text under “Zutaten” or “Ingredients” on verified product pages and exports the results to Excel.

Search details

One product code per line, up to 2,000. The ingredient paragraph is copied in the website's original language.

Use a homepage, search page, sample product page, or URL containing {code}.

Leading zeros are preserved in the Excel output.
